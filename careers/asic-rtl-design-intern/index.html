ASIC/RTL Design Intern

Internship · Hardware · Groningen, The Netherlands

Our proprietary flow generates compute-in-memory hardware from RTL to GDS, and the next step is silicon. You carry one of our designs through the physical-design backend toward a fabrication shuttle, with direct guidance from the founders.

What you'll do

  • Set up our physical build flow on the target PDK and reproduce the current baseline; harden a small trial block end to end as a dry run
  • Harden our compute-in-memory macro to DRC/LVS-clean signoff: antenna, density, fill, guard ring, and SRAM-macro integration
  • Run multi-corner static timing analysis and close timing on the block
  • Integrate the macro into the shuttle's top-level chip harness and close top-level DRC, LVS, and timing, including the host-controller connection
  • Prepare and submit the final GDS for the fabrication shuttle, passing the full tape-out checklist
  • Prepare silicon bring-up: a test-board outline and a validation checklist for the returned chips

What we look for

  • MSc student in electrical engineering, microelectronics, or a related field
  • Coursework or project experience with digital design flows: synthesis, place-and-route, or timing analysis
  • Working knowledge of Linux, with some scripting in Python or Tcl
  • Systematic debugging habits and clear reporting in English
  • Preferably already studying at a university in the Netherlands; we cannot sponsor visas at this stage

Nice to have

  • Hands-on time with OpenROAD/OpenLane or a commercial backend tool (Innovus, ICC2, Fusion Compiler)
  • PDK exposure (e.g. sky130)
  • RTL experience (Verilog or SystemVerilog)
  • SRAM or hard-macro integration experience
  • PCB or board-level experience

What you get

  • Work on a real design headed for fabrication
  • Direct mentorship from the founding team
  • An end-to-end view of an RTL-to-GDS flow

Ximplic is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applications from people of all backgrounds and select on ability and motivation alone.
